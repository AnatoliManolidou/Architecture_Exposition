# Η ΤΕΧΝΗ ΤΗΣ ΤΕΧΝΙΚΗΣ 

**Exhibition:** Installations of Primary Education in Thessaloniki  
**Dates:** 17.06 – 27.06.2025  
**Location:** School of Architecture, AUTh

---

## 💡 Developer’s Note & Project Scope
This repository was developed as a **technical support initiative** for the students of the **School of Architecture** at the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (AUTh).

As a student of the **School of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)**, I volunteered to create this web platform to facilitate the digital aspect of the exhibition. The goal was to provide a seamless way for visitors to access detailed architectural proposals via **QR codes** placed on physical exhibits, bridging the gap between physical installations and digital documentation.

---

The exhibition presents innovative architectural proposals for educational pavilions in the Charilaou district of Thessaloniki.

## 🛠️ Technical Implementation
This is a lightweight, static web-based exposition designed for high reliability and fast loading on mobile devices during the event.

### Project Structure
The repository is organized into team-specific modules to ensure independent access via unique URLs:
* `index.html` — The main landing page/hub for the exposition.
* `TeamX/index.html` — Individual entry points for each participating team (where `X` is the team number).

### Workflow
1. **Landing Page:** Accessing the root directory provides a central menu of all projects.
2. **QR Integration:** Each team's specific `index.html` was linked to a physical QR code at their exhibition stand, allowing instant access to their digital portfolio.
