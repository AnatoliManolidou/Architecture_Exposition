
# Η ΤΕΧΝΗ ΤΗΣ ΤΕΧΝΙΚΗΣ

**Exhibition: Installations of Primary Education in Thessaloniki**

**Dates:** 17.06 – 27.06.2025

This exposition presents proposals for the design of educational pavilions in the area of Charilaou, Thessaloniki. The proposals were developed within the framework of the workshop "The Art of Technique and the Technique of Art," focusing on the integration of technical and artistic parameters in the conceptual and operational aspects of architectural composition. The selection of specific installations responds to the real needs of the local community and is supported by the Municipality of Thessaloniki.

# Architecture Exposition

This project is a simple web-based exposition showcasing the work of multiple teams, as part of the exhibition "Η ΤΕΧΝΗ ΤΗΣ ΤΕΧΝΙΚΗΣ". Each team has its own folder containing an `index.html` file that presents their project or contribution.

## Project Structure

- `index.html` — Main landing page for the exposition.
- `TeamX/index.html` — Individual team pages (replace `X` with the team number).

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser to access the main exposition page.
2. Navigate to each team's folder and open their `index.html` to view their specific project.
